Hi, I'm Ayush, a Senior Software Engineer at Google with 6 years of experience in cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, and agentic AI.

At Google, I've spent the last few years working across two distinct problem spaces. In the Anthos org, I built Kubernetes controllers that helped enterprises migrate legacy workloads to GKE. More recently, I have been part of the Application Design Center team right from its concept stage as one of its earliest engineers to its GA, building services that let users design secure application infrastructure and deploy it at scale on GCP.

Before Google, I worked at Sprinklr on NLP projects which is where I first got hooked on systems that understand intent, not just instructions.

Currently thinking a lot about distributed systems internals, cloud-native architecture, and where agentic AI fits into production infrastructure. Always interested in conversations at the intersection of these.
